# Privacy Policy & Terms

This is a personal, non-commercial project used only by its author.

- It accesses only the author's own Oura account, read-only, via OAuth2. Other Oura accounts are refused.
- Data is fetched on demand when Claude calls a tool and passed straight to Claude; it is not stored, sold or shared.
- OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in the author's Cloudflare account and can be revoked at any time from the Oura account settings.

Provided as-is, with no warranty.
